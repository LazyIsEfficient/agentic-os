## Section 1: Warmup (5 minutes)

> Friendly, low-stakes. Ease in. Establish "no wrong answers."

- "Thanks so much for joining today. How's your `<morning/afternoon>` going?"
- "Before we get into things, I want to set a few expectations:"
  - There are no right or wrong answers. We're trying to understand how people like you do `<topic>`.
  - You can ask me to repeat or clarify any question.
  - You can pass on anything you don't want to answer.
  - You can stop the session at any time.
  - I'm going to start recording now — is that okay?

> [Start recording. Restate consent on the recording.]

- "Just to confirm on the recording: you're okay with us recording this conversation, using it internally for research, and you know you can ask me to stop at any time?"

---

## Section 2: Context-setting (5–10 minutes)

> Get to know who they are. Calibrate everything that comes after.

- "Could you tell me a bit about what you do?"
- "What does a typical `<day/week>` look like for you?"
- "Who do you work with? What does your team look like?"
- "How long have you been doing this?"

**Probe ladder reminders:**
- Echo / paraphrase: repeat their last words.
- Open follow-up: "Tell me more about that."
- Specific deepening: "What was that like? Walk me through it."

## Section 4: Closing (5 minutes)

> Wind down. Two crucial questions.

- "Is there anything I should have asked you that I didn't?"
- "Is there anyone else you'd recommend I talk to about `<topic>`?"
- "Any final thoughts before we wrap up?"

## Things NOT to ask

> Reminders for the moderator. Don't drift here.

- ❌ "Would you use this feature?" (hypothetical)
- ❌ "Don't you find this confusing?" (leading)
- ❌ "How do you feel about our product?" (too broad)
- ❌ "What features would you want?" (asks user to design)
- ❌ "Was that easy?" (closed, leading)

## Reminders for the moderator

- Talk no more than 20% of the time.
- After every question, count to 5 before filling silence.
- When they say something interesting, echo it: "You said `<x>`..."
- Don't defend the design. Listen.
- Don't help. Watch them struggle.
- Note quotes with timestamps as you go.
- Take a 10-minute break between back-to-back sessions.
