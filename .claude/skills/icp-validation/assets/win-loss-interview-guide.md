# Win-Loss Interview Guide

Fillable buyer-side script. Run with the **buyer**, not the rep. One guide per
interview; pick the cohort. Keep it conversational — these are prompts, not a
questionnaire. Method and synthesis live in
[../references/win-loss-method.md](../references/win-loss-method.md).

---

**Account:** ______  **Cohort:** won / lost / churned  **Days since close:** ______
**Interviewee role:** ______  **Deal owner (rep):** ______

## 1. The trigger
- What was happening when you started looking for a solution like ours?
- What changed that made this a priority *now* rather than six months ago?

## 2. The status quo
- What were you doing about this before? How well was it working?
- If you'd done nothing, what would that have cost you?

## 3. Decision criteria (stated)
- What did you evaluate vendors on? List the criteria.
- Rank them — which mattered most when it came down to it?

## 4. The real driver (revealed)
- Walk me through the moment the decision got made. Who was in the room?
- What was the one thing that tipped it?

## 5. Alternatives
- Who else did you seriously consider? (Include build-it-yourself and do-nothing.)
- What did they have / lack that mattered?

## 6. Dealbreaker  *(lost / churned only)*
- What would have had to be true for us to win / for you to stay?
- Was this about **price** (couldn't afford) or **fit/trust** (wouldn't buy)? Probe until clear.
