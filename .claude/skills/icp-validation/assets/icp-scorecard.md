# ICP Fit Scorecard

Fillable rubric. One row per account. Score firmographic and behavioral fit
*independently of outcome*, record the outcome, then look for the pattern that
separates won from lost/churned. The goal is to find the criteria that *predict*
outcome — not to rationalize the wins you already have.

## Scoring scale

For each criterion: **2** = strong fit · **1** = partial · **0** = no fit / unknown.

## Firmographic criteria (score each /2)

- Company size / stage within target band.
- Industry / business model match.
- Tech stack / substrate present (the product's prerequisite tooling).
- Buying structure (budget owner reachable, procurement friction tolerable).

## Behavioral criteria (score each /2)

- A clear **trigger event** created the need (not idle curiosity).
- Buying motion matches what we serve (self-serve vs sales-led).
- Displaced a real status quo (not "nothing" — unless "nothing" is your ICP).

## Pattern read (fill after scoring all accounts)

- **Fit attributes present in WINS, absent in LOSSES** → candidate fit criteria: ______
- **Attributes we call "fit" but present in LOSSES/CHURNS** → candidate disqualifiers: ______
- **Driver recurring in CHURNS** (looked right, failed anyway) → missing fit criterion: ______
- **Verdict:** validate / sharpen / invalidate — and why: ______

A fit score that does **not** correlate with outcome is the strongest possible
signal the segment is too broad or mislabeled. Take it to
[../references/segment-validation.md](../references/segment-validation.md).
