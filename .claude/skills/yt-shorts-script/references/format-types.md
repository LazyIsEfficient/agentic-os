# Format Types

## `talking-head`

Face on camera, speaking directly to the viewer. SHOW notes describe:
- Text overlays and on-screen captions
- Energy level or pacing shifts
- Hand position or body cues if relevant
- B-roll cutaway suggestions (optional, marked clearly)

## `screen-record`

Screen capture drives the visual. SHOW notes describe:
- Which app, file, or workflow to navigate to
- Where to zoom, highlight, or pause
- Cursor or annotation cues
- What to type or click — be specific

## `text-on-screen`

No face cam. Words and visuals carry the message. SHOW notes describe:
- Text card headline and sub-line
- Background style and color cue
- Transition or animation style
- Pacing — one concept per card, hard cuts between beats
