# Output Format Template

Always produce two blocks: **60s script** then **30s cut**.

```
FORMAT: [talking-head | screen-record | text-on-screen]

─── 60s SCRIPT ──────────────────────────────────────

HOOK [0–5s]
SAY: "<spoken line — must stop scroll in the first 2 seconds>"
SHOW: <visual direction>

BODY [5–50s]
[0:05] SAY: "<beat 1>"
       SHOW: <visual direction>

[0:18] SAY: "<beat 2>"
       SHOW: <visual direction>

[0:32] SAY: "<beat 3>"
       SHOW: <visual direction>

[0:44] SAY: "<beat 4 — optional>"
       SHOW: <visual direction>

CTA [50–60s]
SAY: "<one line — follow prompt or link>"
SHOW: <end card or handle>

─── 30s CUT ─────────────────────────────────────────

HOOK [0–4s]
SAY: "<compressed hook — same claim, tighter>"
SHOW: <visual direction>

BODY [4–24s]
[0:04] SAY: "<strongest beat from 60s>"
       SHOW: <visual direction>

[0:14] SAY: "<payoff beat>"
       SHOW: <visual direction>

CTA [24–30s]
SAY: "<same CTA or tighter>"
SHOW: <end card or handle>
```

Timing markers are hard constraints. If the copy doesn't fit, cut words — never expand the section.

## Timing rule

Spoken English at a clear, on-camera pace runs **2.5–3 words per second**. Count words, not feel. A 10-second beat holds 25–30 words maximum. Recount after any rewrite.
