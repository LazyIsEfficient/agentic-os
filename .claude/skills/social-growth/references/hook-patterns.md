# Hook Patterns and Closing Questions

## Hook patterns — when to use which

- `🌶️ Take:` — for opinion you'll defend. Use when the body is "here's why" with a clear stance.
- `✨ Shower Thought:` — for half-formed observations. Use when the post is "I noticed this; not fully resolved" — lower-stakes, invites discussion.
- **Bold standalone claim** — for case-study or data-backed posts. Use when the body proves the claim with a specific number or story.
- **Lived detail** — for narrative posts. Use when the body is "and here's what happened."

Don't open every post with `Take:` — rotate. Repeated `Take:` openings read like a content franchise, not a person.

---

## Closing question — patterns that earn comments

Good closing questions are specific, low-stakes to answer, and target the LinkedIn audience's actual pain. Bad ones are vague rhetoric.

- ✅ "What's the smallest team where this stops working?" (specific, niche-expertise invited)
- ✅ "Did anyone's CTO actually say no to this and survive?" (concrete, slightly edgy)
- ✅ "What's the boring tool you'd keep over the shiny one?" (opinion-bait, low risk)
- ❌ "What do you think?" (lazy, no signal)
- ❌ "Have you experienced this?" (yes/no closer)
- ❌ "Curious to hear your thoughts." (corporate filler)
