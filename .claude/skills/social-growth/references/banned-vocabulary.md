# Banned Vocabulary and Constructions

## Banned words (do not use in either platform)

`delve, tapestry, landscape (abstract), leverage, multifaceted, nuanced, pivotal, realm, robust, seamless, testament, transformative, underscore (verb), utilize, whilst, keen, embark, comprehensive, intricate, commendable, meticulous, paramount, groundbreaking, innovative, cutting-edge, synergy, holistic, paradigm, ecosystem, Additionally, crucial, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, highlight (verb), interplay, intricacies, showcase, vibrant, valuable, profound, renowned, breathtaking, nestled, stunning, thrilled, excited to share`

## Banned constructions

- "Not X. It's Y." or any variant — say what something *is*, directly.
- "from X to Y" on no meaningful scale.
- Em dashes — max 1 per 200 words.
- Title Case In Every Heading.
- Curly quotes.
